UCS (Universal Category System) is a public-domain list by Tim Nielsen, Justin Drury, Kai Paquin and contributors: universalcategorysystem.com. Version here: UCS 8.1 (682 subcategories).

Amber dot = entry still to be checked by a person. Film examples and dates are written from general knowledge; report errors to the editors.
